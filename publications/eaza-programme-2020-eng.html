International research and conservation programme “Conservation of the Dalmatian and Great White Pelican”

Authors: Р. М. Аношин (координатор программы)

Евроазиатская региональная ассоциация зоопарков и аквариумов (ЕАРАЗА)

Source: Approved by the EARAZA Presidium, 20 February 2020. Moscow, 2020. Revision 4.

Download PDF All publications

Abstract

The full text of the EARAZA conservation programme. It sets out the participants, the rationale, the aims and objectives, and the field methodology for surveying Dalmatian and Great White Pelicans. Participants include the Moscow Zoo, the Chernye Zemli and Rostovsky nature reserves, the Kuban research centre “Wild Nature of the Caucasus” and Laboratory of Scientific Films LLC.

Keywords: EARAZA, programme, Dalmatian Pelican, Great White Pelican, survey methodology, conservation
